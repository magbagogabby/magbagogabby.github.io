Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Faith In Life operates the Faith In Life Publisher mobile application or website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you. When you use our app and choose to log in using Facebook or other provided features, we may collect personally identifiable information, including your name, email address, and public profile information provided by the authentication service. We also automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with the app to help us troubleshoot and improve performance.

Use of Data

Faith In Life Publisher uses the collected data for various purposes: to provide and maintain our service, to notify you about changes to our service, to allow you to participate in interactive features of our service when you choose to do so, to provide customer support, and to monitor the usage of the service.

YouTube API Services

Faith In Life Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload video content to our own YouTube channel and to read the channel's own performance statistics, solely for our own editorial record-keeping. By using this application you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Through the YouTube API Services we access only:

  • basic information about our own channel (title, subscriber count, video count, total views);
  • the public statistics of our own uploaded videos (views, likes, comments) and their upload status;
  • our own channel's aggregate analytics reports (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained).

We do not access any other user's YouTube data, and no third party's data is collected through this connection. The authorization tokens issued by Google are stored locally on the device that runs the tool and are never transmitted to anyone else. Video content uploaded to YouTube is stored and processed by YouTube under YouTube's own terms.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or remove our access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok Integration

Faith In Life Publisher connects to TikTok through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only with authorization you grant on TikTok's own consent screen. Through that connection we:

  • read basic profile information (display name and avatar) solely to confirm which TikTok account you have connected;
  • upload the video files you ask us to upload to that account as a draft, so that you can review, edit and publish the post yourself in the TikTok app.

We do not access your TikTok followers, messages, or analytics, and we do not read or collect the content of your TikTok account. We do not sell or share TikTok data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the device that runs the tool and are not transmitted to anyone else. Video files uploaded this way are stored by TikTok under TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Authorization tokens are kept only for as long as the connection is active. You can end our access at any time by revoking Faith In Life Publisher in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions), in your Google Account permissions (see the link above), or in the equivalent settings of any other connected platform. You may also ask us to delete any personal data we hold by writing to the address below.

Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100 percent secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us by email at catholicfaithinlife@gmail.com.

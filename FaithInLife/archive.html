Lk 12:49-53

Somebody Lit a Fire in You

The fire in the Gospel spreads by contact — "one tree ignites the next." Nobody's faith started in themselves; it arrived from a person. So the honest question for the reader is not "are you on fire" but "who lit you, and who is waiting for your match?" The application is one named person and one small act this week, in a named place: the desk, the group chat, the shift, the dinner table.

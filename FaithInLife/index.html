The hour nobody photographs

6:42 a.m.Or whenever yours is.

The alarm goes before you are ready. There is a lunch to make, a shirt to find, a meeting you are already late for.

Nothing about that hour feels holy. The coffee is not a ritual. The kitchen is not a chapel. Nobody is watching, and nothing is being offered.

That is exactly the hour this is written for.

What this is

Faith is not a separate hour.

It is the same hour, seen differently.

Whatsoever you do, do it from the heart, as to the Lord, and not to men.

Colossians 3:23, Douay-Rheims

How a day is made

Nothing goes out that the Church does not teach.

Every post begins with the readings of the day. The teaching that follows is read in the Church's own books, never invented for the post.

Then it is written for the surface it lands on. Forty seconds on a phone. Twelve minutes on YouTube. The same truth, at the length the day allows.

  1. First The Gospel and its commentaryThe readings of the day, with the commentary that accompanies them.
